# FUSE Plugin Policy

Flexible User-extensible Simulation Editor (FUSE) supports plugins to allow the
modeling and simulation community to extend the editor with new components,
exporters, validators, workflows, and integrations.

This policy explains how plugins may be licensed and distributed.

## FUSE core license

The FUSE Community Edition is licensed under the GNU General Public License,
version 3 or later.

The FUSE source code license does not automatically determine the license of
every independently developed plugin. Plugin licensing may depend on how the
plugin interacts with FUSE, how it is distributed, and whether it forms a single
combined work with FUSE under applicable law.

## Community plugins

Community members may create plugins for FUSE.

Plugins submitted for inclusion in official FUSE repositories must use a
GPLv3-compatible license approved by PKB Research Labs, LLC.

Before a non-trivial plugin contribution can be merged into an official FUSE
repository, the contributor must sign the FUSE Contributor License Agreement.

## Independent third-party plugins

Third parties may develop and distribute plugins outside the official FUSE
repositories.

Third-party plugin authors are responsible for choosing an appropriate license
for their plugins and for complying with the licenses of FUSE and any third-party
dependencies they use.

Use of the FUSE name must comply with the FUSE trademark policy.

## PKB community plugins

PKB Research Labs, LLC may release additional FUSE plugins as free or
open-source community plugins.

PKB Research Labs, LLC may choose the license for each PKB-authored plugin,
including GPLv3-compatible open-source licenses.

## PKB commercial plugins

PKB Research Labs, LLC may release some FUSE plugins under separate commercial
or proprietary license terms.

Commercial plugins may include, but are not limited to:

- Advanced validation tools.
- Simulink integration.
- Enterprise workflow integrations.
- Batch model generation.
- HPC/cloud execution support.
- Commercial model libraries.
- Proprietary component packs.
- Customer-specific integrations.

A commercial plugin license does not change the rights granted under the GPL for
the FUSE Community Edition.

## No endorsement of third-party plugins

Third-party plugins are not official FUSE components unless explicitly identified
as official by PKB Research Labs, LLC.

PKB Research Labs, LLC does not endorse, warrant, or support third-party plugins
unless separately agreed in writing.

## Trademark

The names "FUSE" and "Flexible User-extensible Simulation Editor" are project
marks of PKB Research Labs, LLC.

Third-party plugins may truthfully state that they are "for FUSE" or "compatible
with FUSE", but may not imply that they are official, endorsed, certified, or
maintained by PKB Research Labs, LLC unless authorized in writing.
